FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website? +
A standard business website typically takes 3–4 weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger projects with custom features or e-commerce functionality may take 6–8 weeks. We'll give you a precise timeline during our initial consultation based on your specific requirements.
How soon will I see SEO results? +
SEO is a long-term investment. Most clients start seeing meaningful improvements in rankings and traffic within 3–6 months. For highly competitive industries, it may take 6–12 months to reach top positions. We set realistic expectations from day one and show you month-over-month progress.
Do you work with businesses outside Pune? +
Absolutely! While we're based in Pune and have deep local expertise, we work with businesses across India. Our digital services — web design, SEO, and digital marketing — are location-independent, and we use video calls and project management tools to collaborate seamlessly.
What makes Maxima different from other agencies? +
Three things: transparency, results, and partnership. We give you real-time access to your campaign data, we're obsessed with measurable outcomes (not just vanity metrics), and we operate as an extension of your team — not a vendor you never hear from. Plus, our local Pune expertise gives you an edge in the regional market.
Is there a minimum contract period? +
For project-based work (websites, branding), there's no ongoing contract. For digital marketing services (SEO, ads, social media), we recommend a minimum 3-month engagement to show meaningful results — but we never lock you into long-term contracts you're not happy with.
Do you offer free consultations? +
Yes! We offer a completely free, no-obligation 30-minute digital strategy consultation for all new clients. In this call, we'll analyse your current digital presence, identify opportunities, and suggest a tailored approach. Book yours using the form above.
